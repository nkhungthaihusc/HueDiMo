# Vietnamese Provinces Database

Boundary geometry for Huế and its 40 commune-level units is derived from the GeoJSON in Vietnamese Provinces Database v5.2.0.

Source: https://github.com/thanglequoc/vietnamese-provinces-database/tree/v5.2.0/json/geojson/46_hue

The commune-level geometries are bundled without simplification in front-end/public/data/hue-wards.geojson and mobile/assets/data/hue-wards.json.
